03 / Company

Made in Kosonsoy.

Sorgo Trade makes brooms from eco-friendly sorghum. The raw material is carefully selected and cleaned; the brooms are bound with strong wire and polypropylene thread.

Every broom is made by hand and passes quality control. A wide choice, individual selection and custom design on request.
