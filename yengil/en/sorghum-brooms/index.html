02 / How a broom is made

From stalk to broom.

  1. 01

    Stalks

    Broomcorn grows a long, straight panicle. It is cut and dried.

  2. 02

    Bundle

    The fibre is sorted by length and colour and gathered into a tight bundle.

  3. 03

    Binding

    The top of the bundle is bound tightly with thread to form the handle.

  4. 04

    Stitching

    The head is flattened and stitched in several rows. The number of rows is in the model name.
