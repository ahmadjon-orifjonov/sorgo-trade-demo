How a broom is made

The number of stitch rows is visible on every broom. It is the easiest way to name a model in your request.

  1. BrushSorghum stalks with seed heads, the working part.
  2. Stitch rowsThread runs through the brush in rows. The number of rows (2 or 3) defines the model.
  3. BindingWhere the brush is gathered into a bundle at the base.
  4. WireHolds the handle stalks together.
  5. HandleNot every model has one.

Raw material

The brush is made from broomcorn sorghum. Where the raw material is grown and when it is harvested will be stated from the company’s data.
