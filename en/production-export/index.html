How a broom is made

  1. Raw materialSorghum stalks with seed heads.
  2. SortingSelected by length and quality.
  3. BindingThe brush is gathered into a bundle.
  4. Stitching2 or 3 rows of thread.
  5. PackingBag or block, then shipment.
